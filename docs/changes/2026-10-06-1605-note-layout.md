# Writing a note: the form at reading width, the patient beside it

Opened: 2026-10-06 16:05 PHT

## What
- **New visit note** and **Visit note (draft) / Amend note**: on wide screens the form is capped at 46rem (about 736 px) and a column beside it shows, in view while the form scrolls:
  - medical alerts and allergies;
  - the reason for the visit (when it's for an appointment);
  - conditions and current medicines from the chart;
  - the last signed visit: complaint, diagnoses (or assessment), medicines and advice.
- On narrow screens alerts and allergies stay above the form and the rest folds behind **More about this patient**.
- The edit page now shows allergies and alerts too; it didn't before.
- `components/note-context.tsx` (`NoteContext`, `NoteLayout`). "Last visit" follows the same rule as carry-over: this doctor's notes, or any at a clinic that shares charts, never the one being written.
- The carry-over banner says "assessment, advice, medicines and height" (it still said "plan").

## Why
Owner: the form took the whole width on wide screens. Text areas over 1,500 px wide are hard to read and write in, and the space is better used for what a doctor checks while writing.

## Tested
- `tsc` (apart from the missing `@solar-icons/react` install) and eslint clean.
- Headless Chrome on Elena's new note at 1920, 1280 and 390 px wide, dark mode: two columns on the wide sizes; on the phone, alerts and allergies, then the folded section, then the form.
