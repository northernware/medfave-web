# Side column: Add forms close on a click elsewhere; primary Save buttons

Opened: 2026-10-06 20:48 PHT

## What
- An open **+ Add** (allergy, alert, condition, medicine) closes on a click or tap anywhere outside it, or Esc (`components/add-disclosure.tsx`). What was typed stays if it's reopened.
- Save buttons are the main action of their box, at the app's normal button size:
  - **primary** (pink) on the plain boxes;
  - a solid capsule in the box's text colour on the red Allergies and amber alert boxes, where pink would clash.

## Why
Owner: Add should close when clicking outside; the Save button could be primary.

## Tested
- `tsc`, eslint.
- Headless Chrome on Paula's note: Add allergy opened, Penicillin typed; a click inside kept it open; a pointer press on the note form closed it, with the text kept. Screenshots in dark and light: buttons the same size.
