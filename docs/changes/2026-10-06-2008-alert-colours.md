# Allergies and alerts: pastel on light, bold on dark

Opened: 2026-10-06 20:08 PHT

## What
- The Allergies and Medical alerts boxes have their own colours (`--alert-danger`, `--alert-warn`, `--on-alert` in `app/globals.css`): soft paper pastels with black text in light mode, bold solid red and amber with white text in dark mode. Severe is a filled label in the text colour. Replaces the `--on-warn` from #163.

## Why
Owner: white text on dark, black on light; pastel and paper-like on light, solid and bold on dark.
