# Breadcrumbs become one back link, to where you came from

Opened: 2026-10-07 12:55 PHT · Updated: 2026-10-07 13:01 PHT

## What
- The address-built trail ("Patients › Patient › Edit") is replaced by one link, "‹ Today", named after the page you came from; it really goes back (`router.back()`), keeping scroll and filters.
- The trail of pages lives in this tab's `sessionStorage` (`medfave.trail`). Opened cold (link, refresh), it falls back to the page above; `CrumbName` names it, and `<BackTo href label />` lets a page say its parent where the address doesn't (notes → their patient).
- A note opened in the side panel over Today doesn't count as a page (`useSelectedLayoutSegments`).

## Why
Owner: the trail repeated titles, used URL words, and going back through it from Today landed you somewhere you'd never been.

## Tested
Headless Chrome: Today → patient → new note → back, each link right; side panel adds none; cold loads of Edit patient and a new note show the patient's name.

## Heads-up
`Breadcrumbs` keeps its name; pages whose parent isn't in the address can render `<BackTo>` (`components/crumb-names.tsx`).
