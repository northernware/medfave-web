# New dev database; the doctor showcase stands on its own

Opened: 2026-10-06 17:43 PHT

## What
- **The dev database moved** to a new Prisma workspace (Medfave Dev, free). The old one hit its plan limit (207K of 200K operations in October) and Prisma blocked it, which took medfave.vercel.app down. The new one was built from empty: all 41 migrations, then `db:seed`, `db:seed-second-clinic`, `db:seed-group-practice`, `db:seed-patient-showcase`, `db:seed-doctor-showcase` and `icd11:import`.
- Vercel's `DATABASE_URL` points at it; medfave.vercel.app is back.
- **`db:seed-doctor-showcase`** no longer depends on rows made by hand on the old database. If missing, it creates Lia (Dela Cruz), Paula Santos and Jnmark Agustin (own households), and patient logins for Paula (`patient2@medfave.com`) and Corazon (`corazon@medfave.com`), password `password`. They're kept between runs.

## Why
The old database was blocked. Most of its usage was app polling (about 20K operations a day) plus a one-day spike on October 1.

## Tested
- On the new database: all logins (doctor, patient, desk, patient2), `/doctor/day` (queue and requests), ICD-11 search, against medfave.vercel.app after the redeploy. The showcase run twice.

## Heads-up
- **Everyone: update `DATABASE_URL` in your `medfave-web/.env`** to the new dev database (ask the owner for it), and restart `npm run dev`. The old database is blocked until November and its data stays there: accounts made by hand on it are gone.
- Plan (agreed with the owner): dev and staging (`main` → medfave.vercel.app) share this database. Production gets its own paid workspace and a `production` branch at launch (medfave.com).
