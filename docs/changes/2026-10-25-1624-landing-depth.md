# Landing page: texture, light and brand patterns

Opened: 2026-10-25 16:24 PHT

**What**
- **Texture:** a fine film grain over the page, and soft light: drifting fuchsia and blush glows in the hero, plus a pink glow under the film.
- **Patterns** (new tiles in `public/landing/`, drawn like the brand's `pattern-heart-plus.svg`):
  - hearts only on blush, behind the hero film and in the apps section;
  - all three on plum, in the paper-trail band;
  - all three tonal on fuchsia, in the closing.

  They drift slowly and fade out behind content.
- **Panels:** the coloured sections are inset rounded panels instead of hard full-width bands.
- **Light that follows the cursor:**
  - a spotlight on the plum band;
  - card borders that catch the light (`.spot-card`), with the pointer tracked by `CursorLight` in `components/landing/effects.tsx`.
- **Live details:**
  - the ICD-11 card types "hypertension" and the results change as in the app (`IcdDemo`);
  - the vaccine chips turn green as they scroll in;
  - a dotted grid sits behind the product story and the bento;
  - a floor shadow sits under the 3D phones.
- All of it is CSS/SVG except the pointer tracking and the typing; motion stops with reduced motion.

**Why**
The backgrounds felt flat next to the best product landing pages.

**Tested**
`tsc` and `eslint` pass. Checked at 1440×900 (hero, plum band, bento, closing) and 390×844. The cursor light wasn't exercised in the headless checks. Not checked on a real device or in Safari (no scroll-driven animation there; the chips simply show their final colours).
