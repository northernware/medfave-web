# Allergy and alert text is tinted, not pure black or white

The allergies and medical-alerts boxes used pure black text on light and pure white on dark, harsher than every other text in the app. Each box now has its own ink, tinted to its colour: deep wine on the pink allergies box and deep brown on the yellow alerts box on light; pale pink and pale cream on dark.

Tokens: `--on-alert-danger` and `--on-alert-warn` in `app/globals.css`; each box sets `--on-alert` to its own, so the severe badge and the Save capsule follow along.
