# Landing page: three.js iPhones, squircle corners

Opened: 2026-10-25 15:28 PHT

**What**
- `components/landing/phones-3d.tsx`: the apps section's three phones in three.js, built in millimetres from the iPhone 16 Pro:
  - squircle (continuous) corners;
  - a titanium band with rounded edges, lit by a studio environment;
  - black glass and bezel, the Dynamic Island and the back glass;
  - the side buttons, with Camera Control in dark sapphire.
- Motion: the phones turn into place as the section scrolls in, float, and lean toward the mouse. Rendering stops off screen. With reduced motion they're drawn once, at rest. Without WebGL the CSS phones show instead.
- three.js is loaded only on the client, when the section mounts (a separate chunk).
- CSS phones (the product story on phones, and the fallback): squircle corners via `corner-shape: squircle` where supported, with a rounder plain corner elsewhere (`.iphone-r0…r2` in `globals.css`).
- Removed the test waitlist row (`test+landing@medfave.com`) from the dev DB.

**Why**
The corners still looked too square, and three.js was asked for.

**Tested**
`tsc` and `eslint` pass. Checked at 1440×900 (the three.js phones render and their screens load) and at 390×844 (CSS phone corners). Not checked on a real device or in Safari.

**Heads-up**
- New dependency: `three` (with `@types/three` for development).
