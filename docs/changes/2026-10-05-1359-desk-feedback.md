# Patient feedback for the front desk and administrators

Opened: 2026-10-05 13:59 PHT

## What
- `/desk/feedback`: the clinic's patient feedback, open to any staff member (`requireStaff`): secretaries, administrators, and doctors in the desk view. It is listed in the desk's menu as "Patient feedback". It shows every doctor's ratings at the clinic, plus faves for all of them, and names the doctor on each rating when the clinic has more than one.
- The page body moved to `components/feedback-view.tsx` (`FeedbackView`, scoped by `{ doctorId }` or `{ clinicId }`). The doctor's `/feedback` uses it unchanged and still shows only their own feedback.
- Patient and visit links on the desk's copy open the desk pages (`/desk/patients/…`, `/desk/appointments/…`).

## Why
Plan *mobile-next*, step C: so far only the doctor could read feedback.
