# Side column: remove is a small × with a confirmation

Opened: 2026-10-06 20:37 PHT

## What
- Removing an allergy or a medical alert beside a note is a small **×** at the end of its line (was a "Remove" link on its own row). It asks first: "Remove Ibuprofen? Only if it was recorded by mistake." `ChartForm` takes `confirm`.

## Why
Owner: the Remove row took space; an × with confirmation instead.

## Tested
- `tsc`, eslint. Headless Chrome on Paula's note: one × per allergy on its own line; cancelling the confirmation leaves the allergy.
