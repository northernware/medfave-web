# Physical examination by body system

Opened: 2026-10-09 14:57 PHT

## What
- **Note form:** "Physical examination" is a list of body systems (Bates' Guide order: General survey, Skin, HEENT, Neck, Lymph nodes, Thorax and lungs, Cardiovascular, Breasts, Abdomen, Genitalia, Rectal, Extremities, Peripheral pulses, Musculoskeletal, Neurologic, Reflexes). Each is one line until examined: **Normal** fills in its standard finding (editable), **Findings** opens an empty box, **Not examined** takes it back out. General survey, HEENT, Thorax and lungs, Cardiovascular and Abdomen show first; "More systems" shows the rest.
- **Note page and side panel:** the exam reads as a list, system then finding; findings that aren't the standard normal in full ink, normal ones quieter.
- **Storage unchanged:** it's kept in `physicalExamination` as one line per system ("HEENT: …"), so versions, the API and the app work as before; `lib/exam.ts` writes and reads it (`serializeExam`, `parseExam`, `isNormalFinding`). An older free-text exam keeps its text box. No migration.
- `docs/api.md`: the line format.

## Why
The doctor asked for "physical examination types" and sent a sample write-up (Bates' Guide): the exam by body system.

## Tested
`tsc`, eslint, `npm test` (25, 4 new for `lib/exam.ts`). Headless Chrome: a note for Jnmark with General survey and HEENT normal and an abdominal finding typed; autosaved; the note page shows the three systems. The test draft was deleted afterwards.
