# Doctor API: a cheap "anything changed?" pulse; fewer reads per Visits call

Opened: 2026-10-19 16:39 PHT

## What
- New `GET /api/v1/doctor/pulse`: `{ stamp, pendingRequests, needsNote }`. It's the no-show sweep (throttled) plus three small reads:
  - the doctor's latest appointment `updatedAt`;
  - the count of pending requests;
  - the count of visits needing a note.

  `stamp` moves whenever a visit or request changes. The app polls this and reloads a screen only when it moves.
- `GET /doctor/appointments` no longer counts visits needing a note on every call; that's in the pulse now (`needsNote` dropped from its answer).

## Why
The database plan keeps hitting its limit, and production is down on Prisma `planLimitReached`. `/doctor/day` costs about ten operations (no-show sweep, reminders, four reads with patients), and Today and With you each asked for it every minute. That's roughly 600+ operations an hour per open phone.

## Tested
`tsc` and `eslint` pass. **Not run.** The dev and production databases are both on the restricted free Prisma workspace, so even local requests fail.

## Heads-up
- Needs the app's matching change, which follows; older app builds keep polling `/doctor/day` as before.
- Dev and production share one free workspace, so dev traffic counts against production's allowance.
