# Allergies and medical alerts: solid boxes

Opened: 2026-10-06 20:05 PHT

## What
- The **Allergies** box is solid red (`bg-danger`, `text-on-danger`) and **Medical alerts** solid amber (`bg-warn`, new `--on-warn` dark text) instead of pale tints, everywhere `AllergyBanner` / `AlertBanner` show (note form, patient page, side panel). "Allergies not recorded" is solid amber too; "No known allergies" stays quiet.
- Inside the red box, **Severe** is a filled label and other severities are outlined, since the coloured badges clashed with the red.

## Why
Owner: make the alerts solid instead of translucent. They're the two things a doctor must not miss.
