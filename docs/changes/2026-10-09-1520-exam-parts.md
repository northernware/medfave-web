# Physical exam: HEENT and neurologic in parts; normals as quiet lines

Opened: 2026-10-09 15:20 PHT

## What
- **HEENT** (Head, Eyes, Ears, Nose, Mouth and throat) and **Neurologic** (Mental status, Cranial nerves, Motor and strength, Cerebellar, Sensory) open into parts, each Normal / Findings / Not examined, as the doctor's sample writes them. The system's **Normal** sets every part normal; **Findings** starts all parts at normal so only the abnormal one is changed; **Rest normal** fills parts not yet examined. Written on the system's line as "Head: … Eyes: …" (`EXAM_PARTS`, `partsToText`, `textToParts`, `normalParts` in `lib/exam.ts`).
- **A normal finding is a quiet line, not a text box;** tap it to change it. Findings keep their box. So a list of normals stays short.
- Note page and side panel: HEENT and neurologic by part, abnormal parts in full ink, normal ones quieter.

## Why
Owner: HEENT nested, choosing which parts are normal; a filled-in system doesn't need a big box when it's the normal.
