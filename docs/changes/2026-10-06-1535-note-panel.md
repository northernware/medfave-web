# Visit notes open in a side panel

Opened: 2026-10-06 15:35 PHT

## What
- Clicking a visit note from the doctor's pages (a patient, the dashboard) opens it in a panel over the right of the page instead of leaving it. The address still changes, so Back, Esc, the backdrop and **Close** all close it. Reloading or opening the link directly shows the full page as before.
- The panel (`components/note-summary.tsx`) shows: status and who wrote it, allergies and alerts first, ICD-11 diagnoses, vitals, history, examination, assessment, prescriptions, advice and follow-up. **Open full note** goes to the page for printing, history, follow-up and archiving; **Continue writing** / **Amend** goes to the full-page editor. Writing stays a full page.
- It uses the same access rule and chart-access log as the note page.
- Next.js intercepting routes: `app/(app)/@modal/(.)records/[id]`, with `default.tsx` and a catch-all that render nothing; `{modal}` in `app/(app)/layout.tsx`; `components/side-panel.tsx` (focus, Esc, scroll lock).

## Why
The owner wanted notes in a modal on the web. Reading a past note shouldn't lose your place on the patient's page.

## Tested
- `tsc` (apart from the missing `@solar-icons/react` install) and eslint clean.
- Headless Chrome against the local server, signed in as doctor@medfave.com: on Elena's page, clicking a note opens the panel (URL `/records/…`, a dialog is shown), Esc returns to the patient page, and loading `/records/…` directly shows the full page with no panel.
