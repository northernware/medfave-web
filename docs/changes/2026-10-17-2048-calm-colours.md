# Calmer colours: near-neutral surfaces, pink for actions

Opened: 2026-10-17 20:48 PHT

## What
Surfaces, borders and text move from plum and blush to near-neutral with a faint plum undertone; fuchsia/pink stays for actions, selection, links, initials and the logo. Allergy red and alert amber unchanged.

| | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas / screen | #fbf9fa | #221d21 |
| Surface (cards) | #ffffff | #2c262b |
| Muted / panel (incl. clipboard top) | #f3eff1 | #363035 |
| Border | #e9e3e6 | #3d363b |
| Text | #2a1f27 | #f4f1f3 |
| Secondary text | #6b6168 (app) / #5f5560 (web muted) | #c2b9bf |

Web: tokens in `app/globals.css` (and the canvas constant in `app/layout.tsx`). The patient clipboard's top uses `--surface-muted`, so it goes neutral too.

## Why
Owner's call after side-by-side screenshots: the plum backgrounds and pink-tinted cards were too vibrant, competed with the allergy and alert boxes, and tire the eyes over a working day.

## Tested
`tsc`. Screenshots of Today and a visit page in dark and light.
