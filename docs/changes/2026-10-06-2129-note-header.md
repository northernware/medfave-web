# New note and draft: the same page

Opened: 2026-10-06 21:29 PHT

## What
- **New visit note** and **Visit note (draft) / Amend note** share `components/note-header.tsx`: the title, then the patient (linked) · sex · age · household. A draft adds "Draft · last saved …"; an amendment adds "Signed · amending keeps the previous text, with your reason". Before, the draft only showed the name, not linked.
- The form always shows a **Visit** field: the picker when there are visits to link, the linked visit, or "Walk-in — no appointment". Before, a draft or a new walk-in showed nothing there, and a linked one read "Documenting appointment".

## Why
Owner: the draft and the new note looked like different pages.

## Tested
- `tsc`, eslint. Headless Chrome: Corazon's draft and Paula's new note, side by side, with the same header and layout.
