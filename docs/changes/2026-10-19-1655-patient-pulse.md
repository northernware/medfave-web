# Patient API: a cheap "anything changed?" pulse

Opened: 2026-10-19 16:55 PHT

## What
New `GET /api/v1/patient/pulse`: `{ stamp }`. The stamp moves when one of the patient's visits changes, or, while they're checked in today, when their doctor's waiting room moves. It's two or three small reads.

## Why
Around a visit, the app polled `/patient/appointments` (about eight reads) every minute. The database plan keeps hitting its limit.

## Tested
`tsc` and `eslint` pass. **Not run:** the database is restricted (`planLimitReached`).
