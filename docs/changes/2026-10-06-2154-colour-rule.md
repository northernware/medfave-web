# Allergies and alerts: colour only when there's something in them

Opened: 2026-10-06 21:54 PHT

## What
- **The rule:** a box gets its colour only when it has entries. **Allergies** is red when there are allergies, and **Medical alerts** is orange when there are alerts. Every other box is plain: conditions, medicines, the last visit, and the allergies and alerts boxes when they're empty.
- Empty allergies is a plain box like its neighbours:
  - asked: "✓ None known — asked and recorded";
  - never asked: "⚠ Not asked yet: take an allergy history", with a **No known allergies** button.
- This replaces the green "no known allergies" box (#175) and the amber "not recorded" box; `--alert-ok` is removed.

## Why
The owner didn't want a wall of colour, or boxes coloured differently from one patient to the next for the same thing. With this rule, colour means there's something to watch for.

## Tested
- `tsc`, eslint. Headless Chrome, dark and light, on three patients: Elena (allergies and an alert), Sofia (none known), Miguel (never asked).
