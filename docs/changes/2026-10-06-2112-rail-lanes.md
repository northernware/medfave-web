# Schedule panel: columns only for visits that overlap in time

Opened: 2026-10-06 21:12 PHT

## What
- On the dashboard's (and the desk's) day timeline, each card is as tall as its visit instead of at least 104 px. Short visits use a compact card:
  - under 100 px (a 30-minute visit is 76): two lines, the status dot, name and action, then time, status and service;
  - under 60 px (about 15 minutes): one line, the name and start time.
- Side-by-side columns now only happen when visits truly overlap in time.

## Why
Owner: back-to-back visits were split into two columns. A 30-minute visit (76 px at 160 px/h) drew a 104 px card that reached into the next slot, so the layout treated neighbours as overlapping.

## Tested
- `tsc`, eslint. Headless Chrome on the dashboard at 1440 px: the morning visits (9:00, 9:30, 10:30) sit in one full-width column, compact cards.
