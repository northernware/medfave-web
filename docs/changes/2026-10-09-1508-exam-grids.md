# Physical exam: pulses and reflexes as a right/left grid

Opened: 2026-10-09 15:08 PHT

## What
- **Peripheral pulses** (radial, femoral, popliteal, dorsalis pedis, posterior tibial; 0–4+) and **reflexes** (biceps, triceps, brachioradialis, patellar, Achilles; 0–4+, plantar ↓/↑) are a grid in the note form: right and left per site, and an optional note. **Normal** sets all to normal (2+, plantar ↓); **Findings** starts at normal so only what's different is changed, and that shows highlighted.
- The note page and side panel show them as a small R/L table, values that aren't normal in full weight.
- Still one line in `physicalExamination`: "Peripheral pulses: Radial 2+/2+; …; Posterior tibial 2+/2+ — note" (`gridToText`, `textToGrid`, `normalGrid` in `lib/exam.ts`). A pulses or reflexes line typed as prose stays prose.

## Why
Owner, on the doctor's sample (pulses table, reflex figure): try the grids. The reflex stick figure can follow if the doctor wants it.
