# Forms and notes centred, like the clinic settings pages

Opened: 2026-10-07 12:32 PHT · Updated: 2026-10-07 12:45 PHT

## What
- Form pages (add/edit patient, household, appointment, document; the desk's too) centre the whole page, title and card, at `max-w-3xl`. The empty and "choose a doctor" states too.
- Note pages (new, draft, amend) centre at `max-w-[69rem]`, the note plus its side column.
- The back link sits over the centred column (`columnFor` in `components/breadcrumbs.tsx`).

## Why
Owner: forms should be centred like the clinic settings pages, not a card on the left under a full-width header.
