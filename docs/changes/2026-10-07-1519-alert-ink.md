# Allergy and alert text is tinted, not pure black or white

Opened: 2026-10-07 15:19 PHT

## What
Each box has its own ink tinted to its colour: deep wine / deep brown on light, pale pink / pale cream on dark (`--on-alert-danger`, `--on-alert-warn` in `app/globals.css`; each box sets `--on-alert`, so the severe badge and Save follow).

## Why
Owner: pure black and white were harsher than every other text in the app.

## Tested
Headless Chrome screenshots, both themes.
