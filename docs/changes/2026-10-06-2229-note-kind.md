# Notes and visits: notes without a visit say why; today's visit is picked

Opened: 2026-10-06 22:29 PHT

## What
- **"Not linked to a visit"** replaces "Walk-in — no appointment" (a real walk-in is a visit, registered at the desk). The field is labelled **Visit**.
- **Kind of note:** with no visit linked, the form asks **Phone advice**, **Results** or **Other** (`MedicalRecord.noteKind`, migration `20261006T1424_note_kind`). It's cleared when a note is linked to a visit, and shows as a badge on the note page and side panel and beside the note in the patient's visit history. API: `noteKind` on `POST /doctor/records` and in `record`.
- **Walk-in hint:** beside the kind, "Seeing them now? Register a walk-in…" links to `/appointments/new?source=WALK_IN&patientId=…`.
- **Today's visit, automatically:** **Write note** from a patient's page:
  - picks their visit that's in consultation today (else one completed today without a note);
  - if today's visit already has a draft, opens that draft instead of starting another note.
- `docs/api.md` also documents `ongoing` on `POST /doctor/records` (missed in #162).

## Why
Owner: notes and visits are separate records but almost always belong together; "Walk-in — no appointment" was misleading.
